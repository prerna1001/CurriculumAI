Machine Learning Education: Applications and Accessible Instruction

01

Real-world ML applications: a data-driven case study

Activity

Students examine curated real-world deployments — healthcare diagnostics, recommendation systems, climate modelling — and trace each design decision back to the data that drove it.

Learning objective

Analyse a real ML application using a data-driven comparison framework, and say what evidence would have changed the decision.

02

Integrating ML into undergraduate contexts

Activity

Students work through two prerequisite-light lesson modules — decision trees by hand, then a visual clustering exercise — and critique what each makes easy or hides.

Learning objective

Evaluate instructional strategies that lower the prerequisite barrier without misrepresenting the underlying method.
